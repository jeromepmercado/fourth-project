THE RISE OF DRAGON BALL

Enter a universe where strength knows no bounds. Dragon Ball follows Goku, a Saiyan raised on Earth, as he trains, fights, and grows alongside friends like Vegeta, Piccolo, and Gohan. Together, they face powerful foes, unlock new transformations, and chase the legendary Dragon Balls. Dragon Ball is a story of courage, friendship, and the relentless pursuit of greatness—a saga of heroes who inspire and transcend limits.

What You Should Know About Dragon Ball

Dragon Balls

Seven magical orbs that grant a wish when gathered. Heroes and villains alike seek their power.

Saiyans

A warrior race known for incredible strength and the ability to transform into Super Saiyans.

Transformations

Fighters unlock higher power levels with forms like Super Saiyan, Super Saiyan Blue, and Ultra Instinct.

Key Characters

Goku, Vegeta, Piccolo, and Gohan shape the story with growth, rivalry, and legendary feats.
