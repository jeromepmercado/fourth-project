THE WORLD OF KIMETSU NO YAIBA

Set in a world where the night hides unspeakable horrors, Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba follows Tanjiro Kamado, a kind-hearted boy whose family is destroyed by demons. With his sister Nezuko turned into one of them, Tanjiro takes up the sword in hopes of saving her and protecting what remains of his humanity. As he joins the Demon Slayer Corps, Tanjiro is drawn into a brutal war fought in the shadows, where warriors face demons born from human suffering. At its core, Demon Slayer is a story of loss, resolve, and compassion—where strength is forged through pain, and humanity struggles to endure even in the darkest moments.
